Research interests

Quantum algorithms for mean estimation

Estimating an expected value is the core of probabilistic computation, from Monte Carlo simulation to risk estimates. Classical sampling needs about 1/ε² samples to reach error ε; quantum amplitude estimation can reach it with about 1/ε queries. I want to understand where that speedup holds and what it costs, and to apply it to problems in finance such as risk estimation and derivative pricing, which my portfolio optimization work touched on.

Algorithm design and complexity analysis

Query and sample complexity, and the gap between asymptotic speedups and what runs on real hardware.

Numerical methods and scientific computing

The thread through my simulation and HPC work so far. I have not yet published or completed research on quantum algorithms.
